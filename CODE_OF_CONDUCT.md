# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, kindness, respect for differing opinions,
constructive feedback, accountability, and focusing on what benefits the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Report unacceptable behavior through the private contact channel on the
maintainer GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers
may remove contributions or restrict participation for conduct violations.
